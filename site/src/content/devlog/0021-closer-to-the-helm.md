A small one. Every time I started the game to test something, I spawned in the corridor next to the mess hall and had to run the length of the ship to the bridge. Thirty seconds, every single time.

So now everyone starts on the bridge, on its lowest tier, facing the window. The first player is a few steps behind the helm. And the holotable has moved from the mess hall to the bridge too, just behind the helm. That's where the crew will look at the terrain and decide where to go next, so it should be next to the person steering.

The test walkers used to assume they started in the corridor. Now they start their rounds from whichever waypoint is nearest, so they don't care where the spawn points are. The walking and helm tests were clean afterwards: nobody got stuck, and the test helmsman walked straight from the spawn to the seat.

## What this does not prove

The spawn points may move again once the real ship is built. This is a convenience for testing as much as a design decision.
