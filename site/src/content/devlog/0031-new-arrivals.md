I bought a big pack of animated sea creatures on Fab: sharks, whales, dolphins, turtles, rays, eels, crabs, reef fish, about thirty of them. Every one is properly rigged, with swimming, attacking, getting hurt and dying. The first eleven are in the ocean now.

![A whale far bigger than any real one passing overhead, with gliders and stingrays below.](/Thalassa/images/devlog/0031-whale.jpg)

## Who's here

- **Megalodons**, 14 to 18 metres. Ping near one and it comes to see what made the noise.
- **Shark goblins**, the ugly little ones, which also come closer when you ping.
- **Hammerheads** in schools.
- **A blue whale** with its calf at its side. I made the whale 60 to 90 metres long, far bigger than any real one, because down here everything should feel a bit too big.
- **Dolphins, sea turtles and stingrays** gliding over the sand.
- **A moray eel** that barely leaves its rocks, and lunges if you ping.
- **Anglerfish** in the dark canyons.
- **Marlins.**

They swim the same way as the creatures I made myself: the seed decides where they live, and the game's clock decides where they are, so every player sees the same animals in the same places. The difference is that these are properly animated: tails beat, fins turn, mouths open.

And my own creatures stay. The gliders, the jellyfish, the krakens and the leviathan aren't going anywhere. The new ones swim alongside them.

![Our gliders and a jellyfish, with Fab sharks and a monolith behind them.](/Thalassa/images/devlog/0031-sharks.jpg)

## One of these is not like the others

I didn't want thirty creatures to look like exactly thirty creatures. So each school gets its own colouring: most as made, some paler, darker, bluish, greenish, rusty, and once in a while almost white. And some schools are **giants**, two to five times bigger than normal: a fifty-metre megalodon, stingrays fifteen metres across, a whale close to two hundred metres long, and an anglerfish the size of a bus lurking in the canyons.

![Stingrays and sharks over the sand.](/Thalassa/images/devlog/0031-rays.jpg)

## What this does not prove

These pictures are from a test where every kind of animal near the start is gathered in one spot, with brighter light than the game normally has. Out in the ocean they're spread out, and in the normal murk you mostly see them when they come close to the lights. None of them attacks yet, even though they all have attack animations: that comes with the hunt. The reef fish, crabs and the rest of the pack are next.
