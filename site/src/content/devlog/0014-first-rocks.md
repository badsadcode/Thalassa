This morning the seabed was smooth rolling ground. I want it to feel like geology: shaped over a very long time, sometimes empty, sometimes towering over the ship. Today it got its first rocks.

## Slab towers

The first family of formations is towers of stacked rock plates. Each plate has a broken outline and thin layers along its edge, some jutting out and some worn back. Some plates are wider than the one below, so the towers have overhangs. Broken plates lie scattered around each tower's base, as if they fell off it.

They aren't scattered at random. A set of rules places them: only on moderate slopes, in one region, spaced apart, and each one sunk into the ground so nothing floats. The rules always give the same result, so everyone sees the same seabed and the sonar charts the same thing.

![A tower of stacked, layered rock plates on a seabed slope, broken plates around it, another tower further up the slope.](/Thalassa/images/devlog/0014-towers.jpg)

## The monolith

And one landmark, the kind I described in my notes for the seabed: a pillar 300 metres tall, standing alone on the flat floor of the basin, bending as it rises. Its rock is in layers of different thickness, and the softer layers have worn back. Cracks run up its face, and huge blocks of its broken-off top lie at its foot.

It stands about 650 metres behind where the ship starts. If you steer back there, the bow lights find it in the dark first:

![From the bridge window: in the glow of the bow floodlights, the base of a layered rock pillar rises out of the murk ahead of the ship.](/Thalassa/images/devlog/0014-bridge.jpg)

And from the water, a little closer:

![A tall layered rock pillar rising out of murky water, its layers uneven, flecks of marine snow drifting in front of it.](/Thalassa/images/devlog/0014-monolith.jpg)

## What this does not prove

Up close, these still don't look like real rock. The shapes are right, but the surfaces are flat colour. Real rock needs fine surface detail, and the plan is scanned rock textures from Epic's Megascans library, once I've checked the licences. It's one family of formations out of the many I'd like, and none of it has been seen from the helm yet, only in still frames. The tests say it changed nothing about walking, steering or the sonar.

## Next dive

Scanned surfaces on the rocks and the seabed, and steering out to the monolith myself. These are intentions, not promises.
