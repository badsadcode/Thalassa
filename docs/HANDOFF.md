## The project

A first-person 1 to 8 player co-op submarine game in Unreal Engine 5.8.4, C++. Studio: Bad Ideas Works. Owner: Tom (GitHub `badsadcode`). Public repo: `badsadcode/Thalassa`. Devlog: https://badsadcode.github.io/Thalassa/ (Astro in `site/`, deployed by GitHub Actions on push to `site/**`).

The owner's design documents are in `docs/` but **deliberately untracked**: `THALASSA_Master_Design_Document.md`, `THALASSA_Devlog_Website_Brief.md`, `Thalassa_Sage_Authorization.md`, and three concept images. Never `git add docs/` or `git add -A`. Stage files by name.

## What exists and works

All of it is tested headless with a real listen host and clients as separate processes.

- **Moving ship (approach A):** a kinematic ship, with pose snapshots replicated by the vessel and characters standing on it by based movement. Corrections are near zero with 2 to 4 players at normal ping. See `MOVING_SHIP_SPIKE.md`.
- **Fixes that must stay:** all hull collision in one component (`HullCollision`); `p.NetServerMaxMoveDeltaTimeScalar=1` in `DefaultEngine.ini`; the client ship clock taken from snapshots; rotation rates sent as an `FVector`, not an `FRotator`.
- **Soft crew collision:** capsules ignore each other, and the controlling client adds a small movement input away from anyone within 90 cm.
- **Airlock and swimming:** water is decided by ship compartment, and swimming is `MOVE_Flying`. The base is kept in the flooded chamber and dropped past the outer door. Doors stop being solid as soon as they start opening. The scripted diver completes repeated EVAs at up to 238 ms ping.
- **Grey main deck,** the editor default: about 280 m, with a ring corridor, sunken mess, tiered bridge, engineering hall with stairs (hidden ramp) and a ladder (`MOVE_Flying` climbing zone), and the airlock on the hull. It is deliberately unpolished, because the owner said the art will replace it. `-ThalassaTestRig` gives the old three-room rig, and the motion tests use it by default.
- **Sonar:** **N** toggles it, and **Tab** shows the chart. 48 beams, 25 m world-fixed cells, replicated, saved to `Saved/SaveGames/ShipSurvey.sav`. Host and client charts are identical, even with packet loss.
- **Ladders on a moving ship (fixed):** anyone reaching a ladder while the ship moved was thrown astern, because the switch to climbing counted the ship's velocity twice. It was recorded as a two-player problem, but the ship's motion caused it. After the fix, four players all climbed, sometimes two at once. Deck runs at about 135 ms went from 122 corrections to 6, and no waypoints are stuck. See `INTERIOR_RULES.md` rule 10.
- **Helm (first version):** E at the console by the bridge's forward window takes the helm. WASD, Space and C drive the ship, R switches between precise and set-and-go, and X is all stop. The throttle lever, the helm screen and the HUD all show the same replicated levers. The ship is heavy: about 30 seconds to near full speed, and a long coast. The old test keys (I/K, J/L, U/O) are gone. Tested with a scripted helmsman at about 26 ms and 240 ms. See `HELM.md`.
- **Full stop:** X at the helm, or the red button on the helm console that anyone can press. The ship brakes to a standstill and holds until the helmsman touches a control. See `HELM.md`.
- **Sprinting:** Shift, at 8 m/s on foot. It is part of each saved move, so the server agrees with the client. Grabbing a ladder slows you to climbing speed, or a sprinter crossed the climbing zone and bounced off. `--sprint` makes test walkers sprint.
- **Stations and the camera station:** helm and cameras share one station class (E to take, E to leave, leave if pushed away). Six hull cameras show on a large and a grid screen at the starboard console beside the sonar. Cameras render only while someone can see them. See `STATIONS.md`.
- **Crouch and slide:** C crouches. Sprinting into a crouch slides: you keep your speed, lose 2 m/s each second, steer a little, and stop sliding below 5 m/s. Space jumps out of a crouch or slide with its speed. Sliding follows from speed and the crouch flag, both already corrected by the network, so there is no slide timer to disagree about. Moves are not combined while sliding. The view eases when you crouch. `--slide` makes walkers slide every 6 s. At about 133 ms: 14 corrections and 1 visible snap of 11 cm in a run, against 9 corrections and none for sprinting alone.
- **Ship Blueprint:** the game spawns `Content/Ship/BP_Thalassa`, a Blueprint of the C++ ship, where the owner builds the ship's look. Everything added in it, or attached to the ship, is made scenery: players pass through it, and the ship's own checks and the sonar ignore it. Show Greybox hides the grey boxes. If the Blueprint fails to load, the plain C++ ship is used, and the log says so. `--art-probe` tests the rule with a solid crate in the walkers' path. See `SHIP_ART.md`.
- **Gameplay spots you can move:** dry compartments, the ladder zone, spawn points, the helm and camera spots, and the airlock are components in `BP_Thalassa` (`ThalassaShipMarkers.h`). They start from the C++ layout, and the ship reads them in `ReadMarkers()` when it starts. Extra ones added in the Blueprint count too. The floor does not move with them. The test rig always spawns the plain C++ ship. See `SHIP_ART.md`.
- **Walkable shell:** players walk on `Content/Ship/Shell/SM_WalkableShell`, set as Walkable Shell in `BP_Thalassa`, instead of the grey boxes. Its source is `Art/Ship/WalkableShell.blend`, with one `UBX_` collision box per grey box (179). The owner edits it in Blender, exports with `Scripts/blender/walkable_shell_fbx.py`, and imports with `Scripts/unreal/import_walkable_shell.py`. The round trip is exact to 0.003 cm, and tests match the grey boxes. Collision must be boxes: triangle collision got walkers stuck on the mess steps. Run Blender scripts with `python Scripts/build/blender.py <script> -- <args>`, which uses the newest Blender installed (5.2.3 LTS on 2026-11-08), or the `BLENDER` environment variable. See `SHIP_ART.md`.
- **The ocean (2026-11-08):** a generated 6 × 6 km seabed (`SM_Seabed`: basin, ridges, canyon, seamount) replaced the pillar grid. The water is murky, with volumetric fog, dim cold light from above and fixed exposure. Five hull floodlights cast beams, and marine snow drifts around each player's camera. See `SEABED.md`, which also has the plan for the owner's seabed vision.
- **Rock formations:** 14 slab towers with fallen plates on the south-west slopes, and the bent 300 m monolith alone on the basin floor at (-500, -420) m, generated in Blender and placed by rules in `create_test_map.py`. See `SEABED.md`. They and the seabed use scanned CC0 textures (Poly Haven, ambientCG): `M_Rock` and `M_SeabedGround`, built by `create_ocean_materials.py` from sources fetched by `fetch_textures.py`. There are also 145 scanned CC0 rock models (nine Poly Haven scans at many sizes, `import_rock_models.py`), scattered by rules: boulders at the towers, rubble at the monolith's foot, rock masses on the slopes, and lone boulders on the flats. Megascans may follow, using the owner's Epic account on Fab.
- **Sound:** synthesised in code, no assets: engine-room machinery (spatial, follows the throttle), hull ambience per player (inside hum and rumble, water rushing with speed, creaks more often with depth and turns, different outside), console bleeps at helm, cameras and sonar, and a hull impact thud and ring on collision. Nothing replicated: each machine plays from replicated state. `--record-audio` records a test host's sound to a WAV. Headless runs can't hear spatial sounds (the listener stays at the origin without rendering). See `SOUND.md`.
- **Dark biome:** fractured boulders, sea pens, tube worms around four seeps, sea whips and glowing bulbs in the north-east, centred at (1900, 2000) m. The life sways and glows through `M_Growth`, drawn as instances by `AThalassaScatter`, with a local darker grade. Generated by `make_dark_biome.py`, imported by `import_dark_biome.py`, placed by `create_test_map.py`. See `SEABED.md`.
- **Sonar look:** the chart is hill-shaded, with smooth contours and adaptive depth colours, a sweep, compass and readouts, and a glow on new ground. See `SONAR.md`.
- **Holotable:** a 3D point-cloud hologram of the charted seabed in the mess hall's sunken centre, heading-up, with the ship marked. See `HOLOTABLE.md`.
- **Unreal MCP:** Epic's experimental MCP plugin is enabled for the editor and starts with it. A Claude Code session started in the project folder while the editor runs can work in the editor. Test processes do not start it. See `TOOLCHAIN.md`.
- **Surface and seabed:** under the helm, the ship floats at 15 m at most and stops dead against the seabed or anything solid, including a pitch or turn that would swing the hull into rock. The helm shows SURFACED and flashes COLLISION. The scripted motion test is not constrained. See `HELM.md`.
- **Sonar station on the bridge:** a screen above the starboard middle console shows the same chart as **Tab**, with a ping every 2 s on the server's clock. The ping is synthesised in code, with no sound asset. The owner heard it in the editor on 2026-11-07. Automated runs are silent (`-nosound`).
- **Packaging:** `Scripts/build/package-windows.ps1` writes to `D:\ThalassaBuilds\Windows`, with `Host.bat`, `Join.bat` and a README. Repackaged on 2026-11-07 with the sonar screen and the ladder fix. Tested as host plus client on one PC: a deck run with the sonar on had identical charts, no corrections and no stuck waypoints, and a frame showed the bridge screen working.

The ladder fix changed the same switch where the water starts: on LAN the largest dive correction fell from 17.5 to 8.7 cm, but at 238 ms the results barely moved (209 corrections, then 194).

4. **Occasional frame hitches of 100 to 190 ms** in headless tests since the seabed and rocks arrived (one deck run, one airlock run, not repeated in the airlock rerun). ## Owner's direction, not yet built

All of this is recorded in `Decisions.md`.

- **Internet play:** playit.gg UDP tunnel to port 7777 for testing (untested), Epic Online Services peer-to-peer for the real game. Friends must never install anything. EOS needs the owner's Epic developer account first.
- **Helm, the rest of it:** steering that depends on ship systems, a real seat, a visible rudder and planes, and tuning by someone actually playing. The first version is built: see `HELM.md`.
- **Layout:** not a cigar. Looping corridors, asymmetry, so you get lost at first, stairs, ladders, and floors at different levels.
- **Sonar:** finer local resolution (1 to 2 m), substrate from the seabed's material, contacts. The look and a first hologram are done.
- **Seabed vision:** see `SEABED.md`'s plan: formation families made in Blender, Megascans surfaces (licences checked), PCG rules, regions.
- **Mysteries:** the Long Call and the Viator are never objectives. Rare sounds need a stable signature identity in the hydrophone archive.

## How to work here

- **Build:** `powershell -NoProfile -File Scripts/build/build-editor.ps1`. **The Unreal editor must be closed**, or the build fails. Ask the owner to close it.
- **Worktrees:** a new git worktree has Git LFS pointer files instead of the map and `BP_Thalassa`, so every test fails with "host produced no telemetry". Run `git lfs pull` in the worktree first.
- **Tests:** `python Scripts/build/run_motion_test.py --name X [--deck] [--eva] [--sonar] [--rtt 100 --jitter 10 --loss 1] [--clients 3] [--helm] [--exe <packaged exe>]`. The full set is `run_motion_matrix.py`. `--eva` is a rig test: the diver's route only fits the rig, so never combine it with `--deck`. `--helm-course cameras` goes to the camera station and never drives; use the default course to test driving. Results go to `%TEMP%\ThalassaMotionTests`, never the slow `H:` drive, because writing there stalls the game. Read **visible pops**, not raw correction size, which grows with ping.
- **See it:** `python Scripts/build/capture_screenshots.py <folder> name=yaw/pitch@x,y,z [-GameArgs]`, in ship coordinates. A spot outside the hull puts you in the water. Arguments starting with `-` go to the game, such as `-ThalassaSurveySlot=SonarCanyon` to load a charted survey. A name starting `sonar` opens the **Tab** view with the sonar on. A name starting `screen` switches the sonar on and leaves the view clear: `screen=90@11200,450,120` looks at the bridge screen. Always look at frames: headless tests missed a black interior and a hole in the hull.
- **Map:** `Scripts/unreal/create_test_map.py` rebuilds `Content/TestMaps/L_ShipMotion` in place.
- **Opening the project:** the owner uses the THALASSA desktop shortcut, which points straight at the 5.8 editor. Double-clicking the `.uproject` fails, because the registry has no 5.8 entry.
- **Shell:** Git Bash rewrites `/Game/...` arguments, so launch Unreal from Python, or set `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` (needed for `run_motion_test.py --vessel /Game/...`). A rewritten `--vessel` silently spawns the C++ ship: check "spawning ship" in the host log. Very long single commands fail, so put scripts in files. Files are LF in the working tree.
- **Owner preferences:** asks before every push, then says "Yes, push it". Wants honest devlog posts, written in his voice, never overstating. Don't polish placeholder art. His usage limit matters, so stop chasing low-value bugs and record them instead.
